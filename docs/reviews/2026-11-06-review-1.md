# Review 1: first playable (M0–M3)

**Date:** 2026-11-06. **Reviewed:** commit `6a65213`.

**Method:** owner playtest feedback, plus a code review of `src/ai`, `src/meta`, `src/ui` and `src/core`, headless reproductions, browser screenshots at 1024×768 and 812×375, and balance-sim runs.

**Overall:** the foundation is strong. The core rules are clean and deterministic; 192 tests, typecheck and lint all pass; the layering is respected; the decisions are logged well. The problems are in what the player touches: army choice, the enemy AI, unit readability, and the progression loop.

The work packages below are in priority order. Each one lists the problem, what to build, and when it is done. `GAME_DESIGN.md` §5, §8 and §10 have already been updated to match the owner's decisions in this review. Treat them as the spec.

## WP1 — Army choice, troop cards and upgrades (owner's top priority)

### Problems found
1. **The player never gets to choose an army.** Megiddo has a `fixedArmy` (`content/campaign/battles.ts:68-71`), so its briefing hides the troop list and the general picker. Every other battle stays locked until Megiddo is won.
2. **The tutorial army isn't the player's army.** Megiddo uses chariots and Thutmose, but a new profile owns spearmen, archers and the Captain.
3. **The general picker has nothing to pick.** Only the Captain is owned. Thutmose is never granted. The cheapest General Pack costs 600 coins, and a new player has 300.
4. **A points budget blocks the "3 of the same unit" choice** (`ui/screens.ts:344,348`). At Kadesh (budget 250), 3 chariots cost 270, so the third chariot can't be added.
5. **Upgrades aren't the player's choice.** Duplicate cards level a unit up automatically (`meta/profile.ts:81-96`), coins are never spent, and the profile doesn't store a card count.
6. **The Army screen has no Upgrade button** and doesn't show cards held or the coin cost (`ui/screens.ts:524-577`).
7. **Battles never reward troop cards** (`meta/rewards.ts`).
8. **Phone bug:** the briefing opens scrolled past the army header. `app.ts:64` resets the scroll on `.screen-body`, but the element that scrolls is `.screens`.

### What to build (spec: GAME_DESIGN §5 and §8)

**Profile v2**
- Change the stored troop entry to `{ level, cards }`, where `cards` is spare, unspent cards.
- Add `migrateProfile()`: v1 `dupes` become `cards`, levels stay. Today `saveStore.ts` returns `null` for any other version, which wipes existing saves.
- A new profile has exactly 2 troop types, **Egyptian spearmen and Egyptian chariots** (the Megiddo units), plus generals **Captain and Centurion**, and 300 coins.
- Winning Megiddo grants **Egyptian archers and Thutmose III**.
- Stop the Age 1 starter units from re-granting chariots (`battles.ts:8`).

**Upgrades**
- Add `canUpgrade(profile, id)` and `upgradeUnit(profile, id)` in `src/meta`.
- Costs come from the §5 table (cards 1/2/4/8, coins 15/40/100/250, ×1.5 Rare, ×2 Epic), kept in the `ECONOMY` config.
- Max level is 5. Spare cards beyond it convert to 5 coins each.
- `addUnitCard` only adds to `cards`; it never levels a unit up.

**Card flow**
- Battle rewards give 2 cards on a first win and 1 on a replay win, about 70% from types the player owns. Draw them from the battle seed.
- Recruit Pack: 120 coins for 4 cards, weighted the same way.
- Daily offer: 3 copies of one card.
- Show cards earned on the results screen and in the pack reveal.
- Check the pace headlessly: a starter troop should reach its first Lv2 within about 2 battles, and Lv3 within about 6.

**Army builder: slots, not points**
- Move army validation out of `screens.ts` into `src/meta/army.ts` so it can be tested.
- Rules:
  - the battle's `maxUnits` is the slot count, and every slot must be filled;
  - any number of copies of one owned type is allowed;
  - unowned types and resting generals are rejected;
  - points are ignored.
- Remove `budget` from campaign battles. Keep unit `stats.cost` in core: it still sets the army breakpoint and how stars count losses.
- UI layout:
  - a row of slots at the top (`[Archers Lv2][Chariots][ + ]`);
  - the owned troop cards below, each with **+ / −** buttons and its level shown;
  - "Power X vs Y" kept as a hint only.
- Show the general picker on every battle except Megiddo. Each option shows rank, traits, skills and a cooldown. The player taps the unit the general rides with (★).
- Megiddo keeps a fixed composition (1 spearmen + 1 chariots, Thutmose for this battle only), built from the player's own cards so their levels apply.
- Fix the scroll reset.

**Army screen (Troops tab)**
- Each troop card shows:
  - icon, name, rarity, age;
  - a **Lv badge** and a progress bar **"Cards 1/2"**;
  - the coin cost;
  - an **Upgrade** button (at least 44 pt). When it can't be used, it says why ("Need 1 more card" or "Need 25 more coins").
  - a stat preview on upgrade (Mel 5 → 5.3).
- Sort order: upgradable first, then owned, then locked ("0 cards — find in packs").
- Hide enemy-only troops that can't be obtained (Canaanite warriors and chariots), or mark them clearly.
- Add a badge on the menu's Army button when an upgrade is available.

### Done when
- A new player can win Megiddo, then pick 3 of the same unit at Kadesh, choose between 2 or more generals, earn cards, and upgrade a troop from the Army screen.
- Tests in `tests/meta/` cover:
  - migration;
  - the upgrade table and its failure cases;
  - the max level cap;
  - that cards never level a unit up automatically;
  - the starting profile;
  - that rewards are deterministic for a given seed;
  - army validation.
- Update existing tests: `meta.test.ts:25` (starting profile) and `meta.test.ts:33` (automatic levels).
- `balance-sim scenario` has been re-run with "best 3 identical units" armies at Lv1 and Lv5, because points no longer cap strength.
- `visual-check` has been run on the briefing and Army screens.

## WP2 — Enemy AI that fights as an army

### Problem (reproduced headlessly)
A red unit next to a friend that is being charged does `Rotate`, then `Hold`, every turn while the friend is destroyed. In a sweep of 41 positions where a strong supporting charge existed, the defensive AI took it 14 times and the aggressive AI 28 times.

### Root causes (`src/ai/utility.ts`)
1. **Charges are only considered from the unit's current square** (`decide` 139-147, `bestAttack` 203-223). `bestMove` (301-339) never asks "can I charge from there?", so move-then-charge is never planned.
2. **Defensive units have no reason to approach the enemy.** `anchorPull` drags them back to their start square, and their attack threshold is 7 (against 2 for aggressive). Their posture never changes, except for `timed` units at a fixed turn. Megiddo and Qarqar stay defensive all battle.
3. **Turning to face and holding beats helping.** Turning removes the −6 `flankThreat` penalty, moving costs −5 for entering a zone of control, and a move must beat staying put by 0.5 (`moveMargin`).
4. **Approach uses straight-line distance, ignoring rivers.** At Kadesh, aggressive spearmen sat behind the river for 4 turns.
5. **No support logic.** Nothing rewards attacking an enemy already in melee with a friend, or joining a fight. Units act in id order, not best-action-first.
6. **Silent failures.** A rejected command ends the whole AI turn (`ui/controller.ts:506-507`). A general in melee never uses its skill (`decide` 135-138).

### What to build
1. **Plan move and charge together.** For every reachable square, simulate the move, then score the best attack from there. Emit Move followed by Charge.
2. **Posture triggers.** A defensive or timed unit turns aggressive for the rest of the battle when any of these happens:
   - an enemy comes within `triggerRadius` (about 3 squares);
   - a friend within about 4 squares is engaged in melee;
   - the unit is shot.

   Constants go in `AI_WEIGHTS`.
3. **Support term.** Add a bonus for attacking an enemy that is already in melee with a friend (larger for flank or rear). Score squares on an engaged enemy's flank or rear higher. Ignore the flank-threat penalty from enemies pinned in melee, since they can't charge.
4. **Path-aware approach.** Measure distance by movement cost from a flood fill, not straight lines, so units route through fords and over bridges.
5. **Act in score order.** On each `next()`, score all units, play the single best action, then re-score. This gives focus fire and lets the pinning charge go before the flank charge.
6. **Shooters keep their distance.** Move as a group: penalise ending more than 2 squares ahead of the friendly line.
7. **Optional:** a one-move lookahead that subtracts the enemy's best charge against the square the unit ends on.
8. **Robustness.** When a command is rejected, log the reason and move on to the next unit instead of ending the turn. Let a general in melee use its skill.

### Done when
- New tests in `tests/ai/`:
  - "supports a friend in melee" (both postures);
  - "does not idle with a favourable move + charge";
  - "defensive posture triggers";
  - "reaches the ford within K turns".
- New `balance-sim aivsai` mode: the new AI against a frozen copy of the current AI (`src/ai/legacy.ts`), across the sandbox and the 6 campaign maps, with 100+ seeds. The new AI wins more than 60%, and the count of "idle while a friend is engaged" turns is near 0.
- `balance-sim scenario` has been re-run (WP5).

## WP3 — Unit graphics and icons

### Problems (from screenshots)
- **Blocks are tall, narrow rectangles** with the front on a short side, so facing is hard to read. In historical maps, units are wide blocks whose long side is the front.
- **Symbols are thin, abstract strokes.** Cavalry "╱", light cavalry "╱·" and chariot "⌀" look alike. A player can't tell troop types apart at a glance.
- **The strength bar and cohesion dot are tiny.** The level isn't shown on the board.
- **Terrain is unclear:** a hill is a plain beige rectangle, a ford is barely visible, and rough ground (small carets) is faint.
- **Maps feel empty.** At 1024×768 the map uses the screen well, but on a phone it's small, cut off, and buttons overlap it.

### What to build (spec: GAME_DESIGN §10, updated)
1. **Approval step first.** Design a **pictogram set**: one consistent, flat, single-color silhouette per troop type, at the same stroke weight and visual size. Types: spear, pike, sword + shield, bow, crossbow, javelin/sling, horse head (heavy and light cavalry variants), chariot, elephant, musket, cannon.
   - Use the `frontend-design` skill if it is available.
   - Build an `icons.html` preview page (dev only) showing every icon on blue and red blocks, at board size and card size.
   - Take a screenshot and **stop for owner approval** before you replace the in-game icons.
2. **Unit blocks:**
   - wide rectangles (about 80% × 45% of a square), with the long side as the front, drawn thicker;
   - the pictogram centred, with the NATO mark small in a corner;
   - level chevrons;
   - a larger strength bar and cohesion pip.
   - Keep the hatch patterns for Disrupted and Fragmented, and hollow blocks for Routed.
3. **Use the same icon set everywhere:** board, army builder, Army screen, shop, pack reveal and unit card.
4. **Terrain:**
   - hills as 2–3 soft contour rings with a light fill;
   - fords as a clearly paler band across the river with stepping marks;
   - rough ground as denser scrub marks;
   - a small terrain legend in the long-press info.
5. **Phone layout:** at 812×375, the map fits its height on open, the buttons don't overlap units (reserve a bottom bar or add safe padding to the camera bounds), and the unit card fits on screen.
6. **Fix the `visual-check` tooling.** `package.json` pins `playwright@^1.49`, which can't find the pre-installed browser. Remove the dependency and require the globally installed Playwright, or pass `executablePath: '/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1243/chrome-linux64/chrome'` when that path exists.

### Done when
- The owner has approved the icon sheet.
- `visual-check` runs cleanly on a fresh clone and every checklist item passes at both sizes.
- From screenshots, a new player can name every unit type on the sandbox map.

## WP4 — Rule bugs and movement feel

### Bugs (each reproduced)
1. **Evade units lose all their MP when stepping straight back out of a zone of control.** The pull-back rule runs before the normal search (`core/movement.ts:235-236`). Fix: skip pull-back for units with Evade.
2. **Turning and then pulling back lets a unit leave a zone of control sideways** (`engine.ts:92-97`, `movement.ts:209`). Fix: measure "straight back" from the facing the unit had at the start of the turn.
3. **The charge preview shows the impact only.** The melee happens in the same side turn, so the preview says 24 losses when the real result is 45 (`core/preview.ts:106-115`). The preview must include the expected first melee round. This also fixes the AI's scoring, since it uses the same preview.
4. **The Megiddo briefing says a flanked unit "cannot strike back", but it can** (`engine.ts:345` falls back to `opps[0]`). Decide which is right:
   - **Recommended:** a unit attacked only on a flank or rear fights back at ×0.5 in melee. This makes flanking matter more, as FoG2 does.
   - Otherwise, correct the briefing text.
5. **Retreat writes the result directly** (`ui/app.ts:197-199`). Add a `Concede` command.

### Feel
- **Small adjustments cost too much MP.** For 8 MP infantry, one square sideways costs 6 MP and one square back costs 8. Add a FoG2-style **sidestep or backstep**: 1 square, keeps facing, costs about 3 MP, not allowed inside a zone of control.
- **Facing after a move.** Default the final facing to the unit's **original facing** when it can afford it, not the direction of its last step.
- **Evade.** Lower `evadeBase` to about 0.6, or make a successful evade cost one cohesion level. At 85% against infantry, skirmishers are almost impossible to catch.
- **End turn.** Ask for confirmation when units haven't acted yet ("2 units haven't moved. End turn?"). "Next unit" should skip units that have already moved and only have Hold left.

### Done when
Each bug has a test in `tests/core/`, decisions are logged, and the duel counter checks still pass.

## WP5 — Balance and campaign difficulty

### Findings
- Unit cost check, with the utility AI playing both sides:

  | Unit | Win rate |
  |---|---|
  | Spearmen | 96% |
  | Skirmishers | 83% |
  | Heavy swordsmen | 77% |
  | Archers | 38% |
  | Chariots | 21% |
  | Line infantry | 21% |
  | Field artillery | 13% |
  | Arquebusiers | 10% |

- The campaign is too easy: 5 of 6 battles are won 93–100% of the time, and most end in 2–3 turns.
- With 2–6 units and a 50% breakpoint, losing one unit breaks an army, so hold-out objectives never come into play.

### What to do (after WP1, WP2 and WP4)
1. Switch `balance-sim cost` to the utility AI. Use 30+ seeds per matchup.
2. Implement the **Charge** tag for chariots and heavy cavalry, for example breaking off after impact (hit-and-run for chariots). This is needed before chariot balance means anything.
3. Starting points for costs:
   - spearmen 65 → ~80;
   - heavy swordsmen → ~95;
   - skirmishers 35 → ~45;
   - archers → ~45;
   - arquebusiers → ~60;
   - line infantry → ~70.

   Re-run until every unit is within ±15% of the median. Exclude artillery as a specialist.
4. Campaign difficulty:
   - give enemies unit levels (Lv1–3 by age) and, for small battles, a higher breakpoint (for example "break at 60%" or "break when 2 units lost");
   - give enemies one more slot than the player where history supports it.
   - Targets: Prologue 80–90%, other battles 50–75%, age finals 35–50%. Measure with "best 3 identical" and "mixed" player armies.
5. **Make generals matter.** Morale passives are close to worthless (+5% morale is about 1 point of cohesion chance). Rescale traits so each general noticeably changes a battle. Make Alexander's and Hannibal's skills distinct (Companion Charge). Unlock the second skill at rank 5, as §6 says.

### Done when
The reports in `docs/balance/` meet the targets, and the decisions are logged with before/after numbers.
