# Decisions

Design and implementation decisions that refine or deviate from `GAME_DESIGN.md`. Newest last.
Tunable numbers live in `RULES` (`src/core/config.ts`).

| Date | Area | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-06 | Movement | A step in direction *d* requires facing *d*, so paths pay turning costs at every change of direction (search over square × facing). The final facing then adds its own turn cost. Default final facing = direction of the last step. | Matches "MP including turning" in 4.2/4.3, keeps facing meaningful, and stops cheap sideways/backwards walking. |
| 2026-11-06 | Movement | Pull back (leaving a ZOC one square straight backwards, facing unchanged) uses up all remaining MP. | Otherwise a unit could pull back and then walk anywhere, which makes ZOC pointless. |
| 2026-11-06 | Movement | Evade units may *leave* an enemy ZOC freely but still stop when they *enter* one. Turning in place is always allowed in a ZOC (not in melee). | 4.3 says evaders can leave; entering rules stay the same for everyone. |
| 2026-11-06 | Movement | Diagonal steps cannot cut between two impassable squares (river bends). Bridges connect only along their axis (the axis runs across the river); units on a bridge can only be charged along it. | Makes the bridge a true one-unit-wide chokepoint (4.9) on a square grid. |
| 2026-11-06 | Terrain tags | RoughTerrainOK: rough and forest cost ×1. RoughTerrainBad: rough and forest cost ×1.5 extra. RoughTerrainVeryBad (chariots): cannot enter forest; rough ×1.5 extra. | The design names the tags without numbers. |
| 2026-11-06 | Ranges | Range distance counts an orthogonal step as 1 and a diagonal step as 1.5 (same ratio as movement). | Consistent with the 2/3 MP costs; avoids diagonals giving 41% extra range. |
| 2026-11-06 | Shooting | A target must be in the shooter's front arc (within 67.5° of facing) and in line of sight. Forest and units block sight unless the shooter is higher than the blocking square; ground higher than both shooter and target always blocks. Shooting does not end movement (except NoMoveAndShoot). | 4.9 says "higher units see and shoot over lower ones"; FoG2-style arcs. |
| 2026-11-06 | Shooting | Flank and rear multipliers apply to impact and melee only, not to shooting. | Shooting into a flank is not a meaningful tactic and the ×2 rear bonus made archers too strong. |
| 2026-11-06 | Shooting | NoMoveAndShoot: the unit cannot shoot after leaving its square this turn (turning in place is fine) and cannot move after shooting. Reload: cannot fire on the turn right after firing. OneShot (pila): one volley per battle. | Simple, readable versions of the tags. |
| 2026-11-06 | Charges | A charge goes in a straight orthogonal or diagonal line within the unit's front arc (facing ±45°; FrontOnly pikes: straight ahead only), paying the turn and terrain costs. Squares on the way must be free and outside other enemies' ZOC. A unit may move first and charge with its remaining MP. Charging ends movement. | 4.2: "the unit moves in a straight line into contact". |
| 2026-11-06 | Charges | (Superseded by "Evade" below.) A charged Evade unit (not Fragmented, not in melee) always falls back up to 2 squares straight away from the charger, keeping its facing, and takes no damage. If it has no room, it fights. | Deterministic and readable; keeps "heavy cavalry beats skirmishers in the open, but they evade". Revisit if skirmishers feel uncatchable. |
| 2026-11-06 | Charges | Push back: when the charger deals ≥ 2× the damage it takes and at least 10, the defender is pushed 1 square along the charge line (if free) and the charger follows up, so the melee continues. | "A charge that wins clearly can push the defender back" (4.6). Following up keeps contact. |
| 2026-11-06 | Anti-cavalry | Charged frontally by cavalry or chariots, AntiCavalry troops counter with Impact ×1.5 and the charger's Impact ×0.5 (pikes, AntiCavalryStrong: ×2.0 and ×0.4). In ongoing melee, AntiCavalry troops get ×1.25 against cavalry to their front. FlankVulnerable adds ×1.25 to flank/rear multipliers. | Without these, knights (Impact 9) beat spears (Impact 5) even frontally, breaking the key matchup in 4.5. |
| 2026-11-06 | Terrain in combat | Impact/melee terrain effects use the worse of the two squares (forest > rough > open). A unit in a ford deals damage as at least Disrupted (×0.75) and defends with 70% armor ("fights at −30%"). | Cavalry charging into a wood should suffer even if it starts outside it. |
| 2026-11-06 | Melee | Melee is an explicit engagement created by a charge contact (not by mere adjacency). Each engaged unit strikes one opponent per melee phase (preferring one in its front arc); a unit fighting two enemies takes two hits. Damage lands simultaneously, then each unit tests once using its summed damage and worst arc. | Readable, and makes flanking a unit already in melee strong. |
| 2026-11-06 | Melee | A charge's impact and the end-of-turn melee both happen in the same side turn. | 4.2: every melee in progress resolves at the end of a side turn. |
| 2026-11-06 | Damage | Damage is rounded to whole strength points. | Readable floating numbers and strength bars. |
| 2026-11-06 | Morale | A destroyed unit triggers the same adjacent-friend tests as a routed one. "Routed this turn" means during the current side turn. | Losing a neighbour outright should shake a line at least as much as seeing it run. |
| 2026-11-06 | Rout | Units that rout flee at the end of the side turn in which they routed, then again at the start of each of their own side turns (after rally). A router uses its full MP, ignores ZOC, cannot pass through units, and heads for its own edge (ties: away from enemies). Reaching the edge removes it (counts as lost). A router that cannot move away while next to an enemy is destroyed; a router that is charged is destroyed. | Gives "flees every turn, destroyed if caught" a concrete timing. |
| 2026-11-06 | Pursuit | Pursue units that were in melee with a unit when it routed follow it up to 2 squares (stopping next to it or on entering a ZOC). Pursuit does no damage. | 4.7: "follows it 1–2 squares … may get out of position". Kept deterministic. |
| 2026-11-06 | Generals | `inCommandRadius` reads an optional general slot on each side (unit + radius + rally bonus). No battle sets one yet, so the cohesion bonus and rally never apply in M2. | The kickoff asked for a stub; this keeps the tests able to exercise rally. |
| 2026-11-06 | Victory | If both armies break on the same command, the result is a draw. | Simultaneous melee can break both. |
| 2026-11-06 | Undo | Only Move and Rotate can be undone (they use no randomness). Any other command clears the undo stack. | 9: undo allowed only if no combat happened. |
| 2026-11-06 | Not yet implemented | Tags declared but without effect yet: Charge, ScaresHorses, Stampede, RainPenalty, Formations, HelplessInMelee, Bounce. Terrain: marsh, village, fieldworks, road. Weather, fog of war. | Not needed for the M2 sandbox; the data model leaves room for them. |
| 2026-11-06 | Balance (anti-cavalry) | Tuned after the first `balance-sim duel` run: AntiCavalry melee bonus vs cavalry to their front ×1.25 → ×1.5, new ×0.75 melee penalty for cavalry fighting a spear wall from the front, and the AntiCavalry counter at impact ×1.5 → ×1.75. Spearmen vs a frontal heavy cavalry charge: 10% → 70% (target ≥ 65%). Flank checks unchanged (cavalry still wins 100% on a spear flank). | §4.5 counter "spears beat a frontal cavalry charge" was clearly broken: spears won the impact but lost the melee that followed. |
| 2026-11-06 | Evade | Evading a charge is no longer automatic: chance = 0.75 + 0.05 × (evader MP − charger MP), clamped to [0.25, 0.95] (skirmishers vs heavy cavalry 65%, vs infantry 85%, vs light cavalry 45%). Failing means a normal impact. The preview shows the chance and the losses if caught. Before/after: heavy cavalry charge vs skirmishers duel 0% → 90%; skirmishers at equal points 93% → 88%. | Always-evade made skirmishers impossible to catch, which is bad gameplay. Replaces the earlier "always evades" entry. |
| 2026-11-06 | Tooling | The greedy scripted policy used by the headless test and `balance-sim` lives in `src/ai/greedy.ts`. It is a deterministic test driver, not the M3 utility AI. Core tests live in `tests/core/`. | Shared by tests and the balance tool; the `rules-change` skill asks for `tests/core/`. |
| 2026-11-06 | Balance (open) | First `balance-sim cost` run (greedy script on both sides, so read with care): skirmishers +62%, heavy swordsmen +58%, spearmen +48% above the median; archers −63%, line infantry −56%, chariots −50%, arquebusiers −43%, field artillery −100%. Not tuned yet: the greedy script walks shooters into melee and artillery into range without firing, so costs will be re-measured once the M3 AI exists. Field artillery is a specialist and is not expected to win alone. | Kickoff: don't tune numbers yet unless a §4.5 counter is clearly broken. |
| 2026-11-06 | UI: facing | After a confirmed move the unit faces its last step; the facing ring then issues a normal `Rotate` command (same MP cost as choosing the facing inside the move). Handles the unit cannot afford are greyed out. | Keeps the two-tap move simple and reuses the core command, so Undo covers it. |
| 2026-11-06 | UI: targets | Charge targets: thick red frame with corner brackets. Shoot targets: a crosshair. Reachable squares: soft blue fill; pull-back square: purple fill with an outline. If an enemy is both, the preview opens on Charge with a "Shoot instead" button. Tapping an enemy that is not a valid target shows the rule that blocks it. | Highlights must differ by shape, not only colour; players learn the rules from the rejection messages. |
| 2026-11-06 | UI: zoom | The camera never starts below the zoom where a square is 44 px. On a phone the 16×10 sandbox is therefore larger than the screen and starts centred on the first unit; pinch out to see it all. | 44 pt touch targets (§9) beat seeing the whole map at once. |
| 2026-11-06 | UI: hot-seat | Ending a turn plays the melee/rout events, then a full-screen "Red's turn — pass the device" sheet. The previous side's moves and charges are then shown as faint battle-map arrows until that side's turn ends. | §10 "arrows show … the last turn's enemy moves"; the hand-off stops one player seeing the other's screen mid-turn. |
| 2026-11-06 | UI: long press | A long press is 500 ms; if the browser delivers the release before the timer fires, a hold of 500 ms or more still counts as a long press. | Found in the visual check: under load the timer could fire late and the hold became a tap. |
| 2026-11-06 | UI: phone | At phone-landscape height (< 500 px) the status chip, battle title and breakpoint hints are hidden, and the preview hides its modifier list. Everything stays reachable through the unit card and the preview. | 812×375 has no room for them without covering the map. |
| 2026-11-06 | Content | `?scenario=debug-states` (every cohesion state, ready charge and shot) and `?scenario=debug-endgame` (one charge ends the battle) exist for `visual-check`. `?seed=N` fixes the dice; otherwise each game rolls a random seed. | Visual-check skill asks for a debug scenario via URL parameter. |
| 2026-11-06 | Review fixes | From the code review before the final push: units in melee cannot Hold (it is a pre-contact brace); a diagonal charge cannot strike across a river corner; melee damage events never report more than the target had left; pursuit stops if the router has already left the map; a unit that routs during the turn-start rout moves flees once, at the end of that side turn. The charge wheel allowance (45°, FrontOnly 0°) moved into `RULES.combat`. Duel counter checks unchanged after the fixes. | Bugs found by `code-review` (medium); each has a test. |
| 2026-11-06 | Generals | Passive traits are army-wide stat bonuses applied when the general deploys (each unit gets its own copy of its stats), scaling linearly from rank 1 to rank 10; the command radius gives the existing cohesion (−0.15) and rally bonuses. Leonidas' "+10% strength" is read as +10% infantry melee. Skills implemented as six data-driven effects (rally, hold fast, heal, cohesion up, hold the line, flank boost); skills that need new rules (Companion Charge, Feigned Retreat, Ambush, Salvo, Grand Battery…) wait for later generals. | Keeps generals data-driven (add-unit skill: reuse effect types). |
| 2026-11-06 | Generals | When the general's unit routs or is destroyed: 30% chance the general is lost (radius and rally bonus gone), then every other unit of that army takes a cohesion test with no damage. | §4.10. |
| 2026-11-06 | Objectives | Hold-out battles (Kadesh, Thermopylae) name the side that wins at the turn limit (`turnLimitWinner`); otherwise the limit is a draw. | Simplest form of "hold the pass for N turns" (§4.8). Reinforcements wait for later. |
| 2026-11-06 | Era units | 19 era units for ages 0–2 re-skin the archetypes with at most ±1 on two stats; each age adds 3 points of cost. Unit levels add 5% to melee, impact, ranged and morale per level. | §4.5, §5, add-unit skill. |
| 2026-11-06 | Campaign | Six battles: Megiddo (tutorial, 2 v 2, fixed army), Kadesh (3 v 3, hold 5 turns), Qarqar (3 v 4), Marathon (4 v 4), Thermopylae (4 v 6, hold 10 turns), Leuctra (5 v 5). Armies grow battle by battle, as requested. | User: start with 2–3 units per side and grow. |
| 2026-11-06 | Balance (scenario) | First `balance-sim scenario` runs: the stand-in player (utility AI) gives near all-or-nothing win rates because only the dice vary, so small data changes flipped battles between 0% and 100%. Settled on winnable data: Megiddo 100%, Kadesh 100%, Qarqar 100%, Marathon 100%, Thermopylae 93%, Leuctra 50%. Most are easier than the target bands; revisit after human playtests and a stronger stand-in player. | Better too easy than unwinnable for a first playable campaign. |
| 2026-11-06 | UI: game flow | The game opens on a main menu (Continue, Campaign, Army, Shop, Hot-seat). Campaign → briefing with army builder (points budget, unit limit, general picker, which unit carries the pennant) → deployment (tap a unit, tap a shaded square to move or swap) → battle against the AI → results (stars, coins, XP, unlocks). `?scenario=` still opens a hot-seat battle directly for testing. | Builds the core loop in §3 end to end. |
| 2026-11-06 | UI: enemy turn | The AI's turn plays back one command at a time with a Speed button (1×, 2×, skip). Its moves stay on the map as faint arrows during your turn. Retreating from a battle counts as a defeat. | §9 enemy playback; §4.11. |
| 2026-11-06 | Tutorial | Megiddo shows a six-step tutorial banner (select, move, facing, charge, end turn, how armies break) that advances when the player does each thing. | M3 "Megiddo tutorial with guided steps". |
| 2026-11-06 | Economy | New profile: 300 coins, the two Prologue starter troops and a Captain. Age finals unlock the next age and grant its starter troops. Retreat or loss pays 20 coins. Packs and the daily offer are coins only. | §5, §8: fully playable for free. |
