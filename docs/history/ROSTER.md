# Battle roster

This is the full list of battles, compiled from the history agent's proposal (2026-11-07) and `GAME_DESIGN.md` §7.5.

- **Main** battles form each age's required path (§7.3). Their order follows history and sets the star gates.
- **Optional** battles branch off a main battle on the age map. Finishing the age never requires them. They give rarer rewards and harder challenges.
- The history brief for each age (`age-NN-*.md`) gives every battle a `challengeType` and says which main battle each optional one branches from.
